# KantaHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

KantaHub is a karaoke songbook app developed by John Rei Bobis Cabuñas. This Privacy Policy explains how KantaHub handles information when you use the app on Android or iOS.

## Contact

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact:

John Rei Bobis Cabuñas  
johnreicabunas@gmail.com

## Information We Collect

KantaHub does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, precise location, payment information, or advertising identifier.

The current version of KantaHub may handle the following information:

- App preferences stored on your device, such as whether onboarding has been completed.
- Songbook browsing and search activity processed inside the app to show catalog results.
- Basic technical network information that may be visible to a catalog server if the app is configured to fetch catalog updates, such as IP address, request time, and app request metadata normally sent by internet-connected apps.

## How We Use Information

KantaHub uses information only to provide app functionality:

- To show searchable karaoke songbooks and song codes.
- To remember simple app preferences.
- To retrieve catalog metadata or updates if a catalog backend is configured.
- To diagnose technical problems and keep the app working reliably.

## Data Sharing

KantaHub does not sell your personal information. KantaHub does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies in the current version.

KantaHub may connect to a KantaHub catalog service, if configured, to retrieve catalog metadata or songbook updates.

We do not intentionally share personal or sensitive user data with karaoke providers, advertisers, or data brokers.

## Device Permissions

KantaHub may request or use network-related permissions:

- Internet access, to retrieve catalog metadata or future catalog updates.
- Network state, to understand whether network features are available.

KantaHub uses these permissions for karaoke songbook functionality. KantaHub does not collect your physical location.

## Data Retention

Preferences stored by KantaHub remain on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or reset the relevant setting inside the app where available.

If a catalog backend is used, server logs may be retained for security, reliability, and troubleshooting, then deleted or anonymized when they are no longer needed.

## Data Deletion

KantaHub does not currently provide user accounts. You can delete locally stored app data by clearing KantaHub's app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

For privacy questions or deletion requests involving any server-side data that may be associated with your requests, contact johnreicabunas@gmail.com.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by KantaHub. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

KantaHub is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through KantaHub, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when KantaHub's features or privacy practices change. The updated policy will show a new "Last updated" date.
